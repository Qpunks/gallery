# Security

## Stay safe

- **Official links** are only the ones in this repository's README and on https://qpunks.github.io/qpunks.
- **Qpunks will never DM you first.** We never ask for your seed, private key, password or any "verification" payment.
- **Mint and trade only on QubicBay:** https://qubicbay.io/collections/20. Any other "Qpunks mint" site is fake.
- **No surprise airdrops or giveaways** are announced by DM. Real announcements go out on X (@QpunksNFT) and in our Discord.
- **Impersonators:** anyone using *founder*, *admin*, *mod*, *support* or a staff name who is not staff is not us. Do not reply; report them in Discord.

## Reporting

Seen a fake site, fake account or scam using the Qpunks name? Report it to us on X: https://x.com/QpunksNFT, or in our Discord: https://discord.gg/TssPemJ3RQ.
